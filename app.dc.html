Connect to Microsoft 365

Read-only. Case Desk reads your Outlook mail and calendar, Planner tasks and OneNote sections. It never changes anything.
1

Put Case Desk online (one time, on your PC)

  1. Create a free account at github.com with the username cherrycoke23.
  2. Click + → New repository. Name it casedesk, choose Public, and click Create repository.
  3. Click uploading an existing file. Drag in everything inside the casedesk-ipad folder, including the ds folder, then click Commit changes.
  4. Open Settings → Pages. Under Branch choose main and / (root), then Save. About a minute later your app is live at:
https://cherrycoke23.github.io/casedesk/

On the iPad: open that address in Safari, tap Share → Add to Home Screen, and open Case Desk from the new icon. On your PC: open the same address in Edge.

GitHub stores only the app itself. Your email, calendar, tasks and notes are never uploaded; they load straight from Microsoft onto your device.

6

How your items are sorted

  • Cases: each Planner plan is one case. A case number in the plan title (e.g. 24-CV-1182) is picked up automatically.
  • Matching: emails and calendar entries join a case when the subject contains its case number or a party name.
  • Discovery: calendar entries mentioning RFP, RFA, interrogatories or "responses due".
  • Deadlines: all-day entries, or entries with due, deadline, statute, file, serve or cut-off.
  • Today's calendar: everything else on today's calendar.
  • Case notes: the OneNote section named after the case opens in the OneNote app. Outlook and Planner items open in their apps when installed, otherwise in the browser.
